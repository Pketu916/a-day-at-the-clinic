# A Day at the Clinic · SPRY

Interactive scroller experience for **SPRY · A Day at Your Clinic** presented at APTA Private Practice 2026 (The Broadmoor · Colorado Springs, CO · Booth #426).

## Overview
- **Dynamic Sky & Sun**: Sun trajectory, ambient corona bloom, and day-to-night transitions.
- **Scenic Backdrop**: Colorado Rocky Mountains background integration.
- **Clinic Stages**: Interactive walkthrough featuring Front Desk, Treatment Gym, Billing, and Owner's Office.
- **Lottie & Progress Animations**: Live metric indicators and animated clinic workflows.

## Getting Started
Open `index.html` in any modern web browser to view the interactive experience.
